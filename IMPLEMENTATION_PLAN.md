# Panipat 1761: Future Expansion & Implementation Plan

This document outlines high-value historical strategy features proposed for the next iteration of **Panipat 1761: Shattered Dreams**, focusing on increasing tactical depth, narrative immersion, and skeuomorphic visual mastery.

---

## 1. Dynamic Weather & Seasonal Attrition Engine
### Objective
To tie the seasonal timeline of Northern India (dry scorching heat to freezing winter) directly into state logistics and active battles.

### Key Mechanics
*   **Monsoon Swells & Winter Cold**: Introduce weather patterns as active tactical modifiers. 
    *   *Heavy Monsoons*: Increase the river level of the Yamuna, locking down crossing points, doubling supply transit times, and disabling heavy artillery squares.
    *   *Freezing Winter*: Increase food consumption and apply daily attrition (health decay) to southern Deccan soldiers who are not accustomed to the northern frost.
*   **Tactical Fog**: Implement a field-of-view fog-of-war on the 3D battlefield. Fog reduces Ibrahim Gardi's French-pattern cannon accuracy by 50% and permits Durrani cavalry to launch surprise ambush flanks.

### UI Integration
*   An ambient weather bar in the TopBar showing temperature, wind speed, and precipitation levels.
*   3D rain and snow filters rendered directly on top of the Battle Canvas.

---

## 2. Diplomatic Coalition & Secret Treaties (Darbar Council)
### Objective
Elevate diplomacy from static trust levels to a dynamic negotiation sandbox, allowing players to rewrite history by securing crucial northern alliances.

### Key Mechanics
*   **The Awadh Pivot**: Shuja-ud-Daula of Awadh was the most sought-after ally. Create a dedicated negotiation minigame where players send emissaries, offer territorial concessions, or send gold chests to sway Shuja from joining Najib-ud-Daula's coalition.
*   **The Jat Withdrawal**: Simulating the delicate relationship with Maharaja Suraj Mal of Bharatpur. If camp followers are allowed to loot northern temples or gold demands are too high, the Jat forces withdraw, draining 15,000 veteran infantry.
*   **Mughal Court Collateral**: Allow players to seize the Delhi treasury or crown a puppet emperor to gain immediate gold, at the cost of turning the Rohillas and local populations highly hostile.

### UI Integration
*   A "Darbar Hall" screen styled like a royal audience chamber with interactive rugs and letters of decree. Players drag-and-drop seals to execute treaties.

---

## 3. Grand Academy: Flintlock Barracks Drill Simulator
### Objective
Add an engaging, interactive micro-mechanic to the Grand Academy where players train and refine their infantry regiments.

### Key Mechanics
*   **Rank-Fire Timing**: A rhythmic timing minigame mimicking 18th-century flintlock reloading drills. Players tap in sync with visual cues to fire ranks in progression: front rank kneels and fires, middle rank aims, back rank reloads.
*   **Artillery Calibration**: Aligning mortar angles against simulated distance indicators to train professional gunners, directly boosting battlefield cannon accuracy.
*   **Formations Customization**: Toggle between *European Squares* (immune to cavalry charges but slow), *Cavalry Skirmish Swarms* (highly mobile but vulnerable to grape-shot), and *Fortress Encampments*.

### UI Integration
*   Interactive black-powder chalkboard diagrams with 2D animated wooden soldier miniatures executing lines and wheeling flanks.

---

## 4. Historical Chronicles & Battle Replay Analyzer
### Objective
Build an analytical review module that records and visualizes skirmishes and campaign progress.

### Key Mechanics
*   **Peshwa’s Despatch Book**: An automated log summarizing every active decision made, resource crisis weathered, and battle won. The book compiles these into a formal "Imperial Letter of State" that the player can download or read.
*   **Sandbox Vector Replays**: A mini-map playback showing the troop positions (using vector markers for regiments) over the duration of a battle, allowing players to study their encirclements or flanking errors.

### UI Integration
*   A hand-bound leather-bound journal aesthetic that opens with paper rustling sound effects, presenting a chronological ledger of the campaign.
